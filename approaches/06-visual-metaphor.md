# Approach 06 — sustained visual metaphor

Choose ONE metaphor drawn from everyday visual literacy and render the
entire material inside it.

## Method

- Metaphor selection is the critical step. Candidates: transit/subway
  map (flows, dependencies, lines = concerns), city/neighborhood map
  (modules, zones, roads = interfaces), river system (data flow,
  tributaries, confluence), assembly line (pipelines), family tree /
  phylogeny (variants, lineages), anatomy (roles in an organism),
  control room (monitoring, states). Pick for STRUCTURAL fit: the
  metaphor's native relations must match the material's real relations.
- Render the metaphor as one large hand-authored SVG scene (or
  CSS-composed scene) occupying the hero position, with a legend that
  maps metaphor elements → real things explicitly.
- Every metaphor element must correspond to a real element; decorative
  scenery is allowed only where it can't be mistaken for meaning.
- Below the scene: a compact "reading this map" section and short
  sections keyed to metaphor locations ("Line 3: the retraining loop")
  that carry the facts and numbers the scene can't.
- Include one honest "where the metaphor breaks" note — a place the
  analogy would mislead if pushed (this protects accuracy).

## Hard constraints

- Exactly one metaphor, sustained throughout — no mixing.
- The legend is mandatory and above the fold or one scroll away.
- No Mermaid (its visual grammar would fight the metaphor); no external
  libs.

## Glance zone

Title + the metaphor scene with its legend visible enough that a
10-second viewer grasps the mapping and the headline structure.

Common myths

“Collages a database” weights hold statistics, not copies
“Cleans a real photo” it starts from synthetic noise
“The VAE generates” it only compresses; diffusion generates
Exceptions: images repeated many times in training have occasionally been near-duplicated (rare failure, not the mechanism); image-to-image mode deliberately starts from your photo.

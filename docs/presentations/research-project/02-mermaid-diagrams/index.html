murmur — cheap augmentation vs a bigger model

murmur bets that near-free, natural-reverb-flavoured audio augmentations beat reaching for a bigger model on small-data bird-call classification; below are the ledger, the two failures, and the honest verdict.

The gain is real but modest and concentrated in common species; the rare tail barely moves, which is why the next move is class-conditional.

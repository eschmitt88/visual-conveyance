Can near-free, natural-reverb-flavoured audio augmentation beat spending on a bigger backbone — on 6,200 clips across 34 long-tailed bird species?

Partially supported: the best stack lifts validation macro-F1 from 0.612 to 0.671 (+5.9 points) at negligible compute — but the gain concentrates in the common species, barely reaches the rare tail, and two family members earned their way out of the recipe.
